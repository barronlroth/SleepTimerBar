# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who use a Mac in bed or late at night and do not mind watching a little TV, YouTube, a stream, or listening to something before sleep. They want the Mac to fade out and sleep without turning bedtime into a productivity protocol.

## Product Purpose

Sleep Timer Bar is a tiny macOS menu bar utility that starts a sleep timer, fades display brightness and system volume, then puts the Mac to sleep. The website should make the product feel simple, cozy, and real, with a clean path to the GitHub repo.

## Brand Personality

Cute, cozy, deadpan. The voice is warm but dry: it gently jokes about sleep optimization culture without naming specific people or becoming mean.

## Anti-references

Avoid wellness-scold energy, productivity guru language, generic SaaS hero sections, complex onboarding, fake metrics, and overexplained sleep science. Do not make the page feel like a medical, habit-tracking, or self-improvement product.

## Design Principles

Lead with permission, not optimization.
Make the utility obvious in one glance.
Use one memorable bedtime image instead of a busy marketing layout.
Keep the joke implied and the product behavior plain.
Prefer calm confidence over feature sprawl.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast or better, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, useful image alt text, and reduced-motion-safe transitions. The page should work as a simple static document without JavaScript.
