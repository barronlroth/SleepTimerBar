Sleep Timer Bar

For people who don't mind a little TV before bed.

Start a timer from your Mac menu bar. Sleep Timer Bar fades brightness and volume, then puts your Mac to sleep when the clock runs out.

Download for Mac View source

That is the whole thing.

It sits in the menu bar, handles the fade, and lets the Mac call it a night.

01

Click the moon.

Start your default sleep timer from the menu bar. Click again to cancel.

02

Finish the episode.

Brightness and volume fade while you pretend this is the last video.

03

The Mac sleeps.

When the timer ends, Sleep Timer Bar puts the whole system to sleep.

No sleep protocol required.
